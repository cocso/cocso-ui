The cocso brand lifts its dark surfaces a step: `surface-primary` `#1A1D21`, `surface-secondary` `#24282D`, with `border-secondary` and the four `feedback-*-subtle` tints lifted to match. The base theme is unchanged, so a consumer that does not set `data-brand="cocso"` sees nothing new; `theme-cocso.css` carries the new values.
