Add `surface-glass-liquid` (white-alpha-30 light, black-alpha-20 dark): the tint for a glass pane the platform already makes legible, such as iOS 26 Liquid Glass. `surface-glass` stays the tint for panes that carry their own contrast (the web's `backdrop-filter`, the SwiftUI material fallback).
